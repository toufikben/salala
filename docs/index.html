Companion

Breeder records that live on your own phone. No account, no ads, no tracking — and it works with the aeroplane mode on.

This page hosts the documents Play requires for the Companion Android app (com.toufikben.ledger). It is not the app itself; the app is distributed through Google Play.

What the app does

Companion is a record book for people who raise dogs and cats: a card for every animal, litters and whelping windows, pedigrees, vaccinations and their due dates, weight curves, health tests, buyers and placements, and PDFs and backups you can carry out.

What it does not do

  • It does not create an account, and there is no server of ours holding anything.
  • It does not show advertising, and it contains no analytics or tracking SDK.
  • It does not diagnose or prescribe. It records what you type and does arithmetic on the dates and weights you give it. Your vet is still your vet.
