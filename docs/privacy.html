Companion — Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · Applies to the Companion Android app (com.toufikben.ledger)

The short version. Companion keeps your animals' records on your own phone. There is no account, no server of ours, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. We do not receive your records, your photographs or anything about you. Nothing leaves your device unless you export it yourself.

1. Who we are

Companion is published by an individual developer. For any question about this policy or about your data, write to www.toufik.bendjeddah@gmail.com. This is a real inbox that is read.

2. What the app stores, and where

Everything the app records is written to a database file inside the app's own private storage area on your device, and to image files in the same area. That data is:

  • Animals you add: name, species, breed, sex, date of birth, colour, markings, registration and microchip numbers, and any notes you type.
  • Records you add about them: vaccinations and due dates, weights, health tests and results, vet visits, symptoms, matings, litters, puppies, placements, buyers, and amounts of money you choose to record.
  • Photographs you attach, and contract files you attach to a placement.
  • Your app settings: chosen language, PIN (stored as a salted hash in the platform's secure storage, never as the PIN itself), and whether you have seen the introduction.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. The app has no networking code that sends your records out, and no server to send them to.

4. Permissions the app asks for, and why

  • Notifications — to remind you that a vaccination, a repeat dose, a screening or a whelping date is due. You can refuse this and the app still works; you simply will not be reminded.
  • Camera and photo access (only when you tap to add a photograph) — to attach a picture to an animal. The app receives only the images you pick.
  • File access through the system file picker (only when you export or import) — so you can save a backup or a PDF where you want it, and restore one you saved earlier.

Every one of these is optional. Denying any of them leaves the records themselves untouched.

5. When data leaves your device

Only when you make it leave, and only to where you send it:

  • Export / backup — creates a file you choose the destination of, using the system file picker. Where it goes afterwards (a cloud drive, a messaging app, a printer) is decided by you and governed by that service's own privacy policy.
  • Share — sends a PDF, spreadsheet or backup through the Android share sheet to an app you pick. Nothing is shared automatically.

We are not a party to any of these transfers and receive no copy.

6. Purchases

If you buy a subscription, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your payment details, your address or your card. We receive only what Google Play provides to every developer: whether a subscription is active, and the product identifier. Google's handling of your payment data is described in Google's own privacy policy.

7. Children

Companion is a record-keeping tool for adults who keep and breed animals. It is not directed at children, and it collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

8. Your rights, and how to exercise them

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or erase on your behalf. You exercise every right directly, in the app:

  • Access and portability — export a full backup, or a CSV of any table, at any time, from Settings.
  • Erasure — delete an animal or a record and it is removed from the database immediately; delete the app and every file it created goes with it.
  • Objection and restriction — turn off notifications, refuse any permission, or stop using the app. None of these degrades data you already have.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Brazil, California or any other jurisdiction with data-protection law: the app processes no personal data on our side, so there is no processing to which those rights would attach. The records on your phone are yours and remain under your sole control.

9. Security

Your records are protected by your device's own storage encryption and by the Android app sandbox, which prevents other apps from reading this app's private files. The optional PIN lock additionally gates the app itself; the PIN is stored as a salted hash in the platform keystore-backed secure storage, never in plain text. Android's automatic cloud backup is explicitly disabled for the database, the photographs and the contract files, so a phone backup made by the operating system does not carry your records to Google's servers.

No system is perfectly secure. If your device is lost or shared, the PIN lock and your device's own screen lock are the protections that matter.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app begins to collect anything, this page will say so before that version ships, the change will be described in the app's release notes, and the effective date above will change. A version that collects data will ask you first.

لا شيء. لا نجمع سجلاتك ولا صورك ولا اسمك ولا بريدك ولا موقعك ولا جهات اتصالك ولا معرّف الإعلانات ولا تقارير الأعطال. التطبيقة لا تحتوي أي حزمة تحليلات أو تتبّع.

الأذونات ولماذا

  • الإشعارات — لتذكيرك بموعد تطعيم أو جرعة أو فحص أو ولادة. رفضها لا يمنع التطبيقة من العمل، لكنك لن تُذكَّر.

كلها اختيارية، ورفض أيٍّ منها لا يمسّ سجلاتك.
